If it is an emergency

Shut the main first, then call.

If water is running where it should not be, close the main valve before anything else — usually at the meter or where the supply enters the house. Then open a low faucet to drain the line and call the shop.

On a Saturday or Sunday the phone is on around the clock. On a weekday the shop runs 8 AM to 4 PM. Do not wait for morning.
