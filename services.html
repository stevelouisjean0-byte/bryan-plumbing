What we do

Three trades, one crew, one clean handoff.

Most repipes involve a plumber, then a drywall contractor, then a scheduling problem between them. We carry all three parts of the job ourselves.

01 · The specialty

Whole-home repipes

The job we are known for and the one our reviews mention most. Fair price, on-time start, on-time finish, and clear communication from the first walkthrough to the final inspection.

Old supply lines come out, new lines go in, and the system is pressure-tested and inspected before a single wall closes. You get a schedule at the walkthrough, and our reviews will tell you we keep to it.

02 · Day to day

Repairs & service

Leaks, stoppages, and the failures that never pick a convenient hour. We answer, we schedule quickly, and we act fast on repairs — the shop runs 8 to 4 on weekdays, and the phone is on around the clock at weekends.

If water is going somewhere it should not, do not wait for morning. Shut the main if you can find it, then call.

Leaks & burst lines Stoppages Water heaters Slab leak detection

03 · The part others skip

Drywall, closed behind us

A repipe opens walls, and most plumbers leave them open. Our own drywall crew follows the pipe work, so the house is made whole again without hiring a second contractor and chasing two schedules.

Patch, texture, and paint-ready walls you would never guess were open a week ago. It is the part of the job customers mention most after the plumbing itself.
