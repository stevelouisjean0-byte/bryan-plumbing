Common questions

About the schedule.

How long does a whole-home repipe take?

It depends on the house, so we commit to a schedule at the walkthrough — and our reviews will tell you we keep to it.

Do you patch the walls after a repipe?

Yes. Our own drywall crew follows the pipe work and closes everything the repipe opened — it is the part of the job customers mention most after the plumbing itself.

Do I need to move out during the work?

Ask us at the walkthrough. We plan the stages around the house so water is off for as little of the day as the job allows.
